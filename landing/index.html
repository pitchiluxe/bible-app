Monthly Reading Plan

Every month opens with a theme, a verse to carry through it, and a reading for every single day — right in the Study room.

  • Genesis for January's new start, Proverbs by date in May, psalms of thanksgiving in November
  • Isaiah's promises through Advent — and Luke 2 on Christmas Day
  • A word of encouragement for each week, a calendar of the month, and a gentle count of days to catch up
New
🎙️

Natural Reading Voice

Listen to scripture read by a warm, human-sounding voice instead of a robotic one — on your own computer, fully offline.

  • Five voices to choose from — American and British, warm or deeper
  • Reads like a person: pauses at commas and full stops, rests after the reference, slows to land the last line
  • Ready on its own: it downloads once in the background (about 165 MB) the first time you open the Bible, and nothing you read ever leaves your device
